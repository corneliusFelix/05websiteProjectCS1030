Who we are:

First of all, we'd like to acknowledge that we are, in fact, not a "we" but rather an "I." Just a single individual—but for editorial purposes, "we" sounds better, doesn't it? I think people are more likely to pay attention when they think there's a whole team behind something as opposed to just one person; however, I have no data to back this up... but WE do! The data is solid.

Haha, no it isn't. Don't believe random strangers on the internet.

We'd also like to disclose our use of AI on this website; it was used for the writing of the cases. Even though we already had some knowledge about the events mentioned, we didn't want to miss or add anything that wasn't factual, so we asked the free version of Claude for a summary of the events from official sources and ran it through the free version of Gemini for fact-checking.

The prompt used for fact-checking on Gemini was: "You are a top editor at Reuters. Someone hands you their summary of (EVENT HERE); your job is to fact-check it and make sure it's all good for publication."

The free version of ChatGPT was also used to figure out why this website was shifting slightly to the right from one page to the next. It turns out that because some pages were shorter, the browser didn't need to add a scrollbar, which caused a slight layout shift when switching pages. The solution is to force-add a scrollbar, even if it may not be needed, to prevent that behavior.
